How Public Assistance & Grant Pathways Work

Programs vary depending on whether you are applying as an individual household or as a registered organization:

Individuals & Households

Direct assistance for individuals—such as housing, utilities, food, healthcare, or education support—is typically offered through public benefit and assistance programs.

Nonprofits, Schools & Entities

Federal grants listed on Grants.gov generally support registered organizations, municipalities, tribes, and institutions that register on SAM.gov and follow program guidelines.

Key Steps for Applicants

  1. Confirm Eligibility & Program Type: Check whether the support you need is an individual benefit program or an organizational grant opportunity.
  2. Complete Required Registrations: Organizations applying for federal grants register on SAM.gov and set up a Grants.gov account.
  3. Review Requirements & Deadlines: Carefully read program rules, documentation needs, and submission timelines before applying.
  4. Use Official .gov Channels: Submit applications and verify status directly through authorized government portals.
