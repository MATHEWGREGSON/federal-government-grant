How Federal Grants & Assistance Work

Federal funding programs support public priorities through grants, cooperative agreements, loans, and assistance listings. Most federal grants are awarded to eligible organizations—such as nonprofits, schools, and local agencies—rather than directly to individuals.

  • Individuals & Families: Look for personal benefits, financial assistance, and aid programs through official government portals.
  • Organizations & Entities: Review eligibility rules, register required entity records, and track open funding announcements before applying.
  • Free Official Forms: Official government information and application portals do not charge fees just to view or download standard forms.

Official Government Portals

Use these official federal websites to search programs, check eligibility, and read primary agency rules:

  • Grants.gov — Search federal grant opportunities and review applicant guidance.
  • SAM.gov Federal Assistance — Browse federal assistance listings and program details across agencies.
  • Grants.gov Program Overview & Personal Benefits Links — Compare federal grant programs and find guidance for personal benefits.
