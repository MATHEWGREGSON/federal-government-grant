How Official Federal Grant Applications Work

Organizations seeking discretionary federal grant funding must complete several official steps directly through authorized U.S. government portals before submitting an application:

  1. Obtain a Unique Entity ID (UEI) & Register on SAM.gov: Organizations applying as prime recipients must register their entity at SAM.gov to receive a Unique Entity ID and designate an Electronic Business Point of Contact (EBiz POC).
  2. Create a Grants.gov Workspace Account: Once SAM.gov registration is active, authorized representatives register on Grants.gov using their organization's UEI to access application packages.
  3. Review Notice of Funding Opportunity (NOFO) Rules: Every grant program outlines strict eligibility criteria, matching requirements, reporting rules, and submission deadlines.
  4. Maintain Post-Award Compliance: Grant recipients must follow federal procurement, financial management, and audit requirements (including Single Audit rules where applicable).
